Target compound: 2a (J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2000, 122, 12013)

To a solution of Pd2(dba)3-CHCl3 (0.5 mol-%) and tri-2-furylphosphine (2 mol-%) in THF (1.5 mL) were added iodobenzene (0.55 mmol), 2-pyridyldimethyl(vinyl)silane (0.50 mmol) and triethylamine (0.60 mmol) at room temperature under argon and the reaction mixture was stirred at 50 °C for 24 h. After cooling to room temperature, toluene (5 mL) was added to the reaction mixture. This mixture was extracted with 1 M aq HCl (6 x 10 mL). The combined aquous phase was neturalized by adding NaHCO3 and then was extracted with EtOAc (3 x 30 mL). Drying over Na2SO4 and removal of the solvents under reduced pressure afforded the product (111 mg, 93 %) as a pale yellow  oil.
