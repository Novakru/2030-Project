A. 2,6-Di-tert-butyl-4-methylpyrylium trifluoromethanesulfonate (1). The apparatus was purged with dry nitrogen, the nitrogen flow was stopped, and to the flask were added (304.6 mL, 300 g, 2.5 mol) of pivaloyl chloride and (59.35 mL, 46 g, 0.62 mol) of anhydrous tert-butyl alcohol (Note 2). The reaction mixture was warmed to 85°C. Heating was discontinued. Then (109 mL, 187.5 g, 1.25 mol) of trifluoromethanesulfonic acid was added with stirring during a period of 2–3 min (Note 3). After the addition was complete, the temperature was maintained at 95–105°C for 10 min. The brown reaction mixture was cooled to −10°C. Diethyl ether (1 L) (Note 4) was added. The precipitate  was collected by filtration, washed with three 300-mL portions of diethyl ether, and air-dried on a medium-porosity fritted-glass filter to give 118–137 g (53–62%) of light tan 2,6-di-tert-butyl-4-methylpyrylium trifluoromethanesulfonate, mp 153–164°C (Note 5).
