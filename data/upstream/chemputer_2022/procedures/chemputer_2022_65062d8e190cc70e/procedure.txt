A reactor was charged with copper iodide  (1.89 g, 9.9 mmol) (Note 15). The flask was evacuated and refilled with argon three times. Then 30 mL of tetrahydrofuran (THF) (Note 16) was added to the flask, stirring was initiated, and the slurry was cooled to −30°C with a dry ice-bromobenzene bath. A solution of phenylmagnesium bromide in THF (99 mL, 1 M, 18.0 g, 0.099 mol) (Note 17) was added over a period of 10 min, and the stirred slurry was stirred for 10 min.  Then a solution of (S,S)- 1,2,3,4-diepoxybutane  in THF (11 mL, 3 M, 2.84 g, 0.033 mol) was added dropwise via syringe over a period of 10 min. The reaction was stirred for 10 min at −30°C. Then the reaction mixture was warmed to 0°C with an ice-water bath for 2.5 hr. Then a solution of saturated aqueous ammonium chloride solution ( 200 mL) was added to quench the reaction.  The reaction was stirred for 15 min at room temperature yielding a deep blue color.  The aqueous layer was extracted with DCM (4 × 50 mL). The combined organic layers were dried over MgSO4 and concentrated in vacuo. The material was dried at 50 °C for 30 min.
