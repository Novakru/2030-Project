To  a  Schlenk  tube  with  screw-cap  was  added 
NiBr2·glyme(5mol%), dtbbpy(6mol%),  and aryl  iodide  (0.15  mmol,  if  solid). 
The  tube  was  evacuated  and  back-filled  with  nitrogen (repeat  this  process  three  times)  
and THF  (1  mL)was added  via  syringe.  
The  resulting mixture  was stirred  at  room  temperature  for  5  min.  
Then  aryl  iodide (0.15mmol,  if  liquid), alkene  (0.1  mmol), alkyl  iodide  (0.3  mmol),  and  TDAE (0.3mmol) were  added  sequentially via  syringe.
The  mixture was  stirred  at  room temperatureand  conversion  was monitored by TLC. 
The  mixture  was  quenched  with water(3  mL) and diluted  with  ethyl  acetate(25  mL). 
The  organic  phase  was  washed  with  NH4Cl (sat. solution)and  brine,  dried  over  anhydrous magnesium sulfate, filtered and concentrated under reduced pressure. 
The crude product was purified by column chromatography on silica gel with hexane:ethyl acetate mixtures as eluent to give the corresponding products in pure form.
