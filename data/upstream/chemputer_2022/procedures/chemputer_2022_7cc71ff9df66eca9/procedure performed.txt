"Into a 250-mL, round-bottomed flask equipped with a magnetic stir bar is added 1-Octyne(5 g, 45.37 mmol) and 107 mL of acetone. The flask is equipped with a rubber septum and argon inlet needle. N-Bromosuccinimide (8.88 g, 49.9 mmol, 1.1 equiv) and silver nitrate (0.770 g, 4.53 mmol, 0.10 equiv) were added in portion through temporary removal of the septum. The rubber septum is replaced with an argon inlet adapter, the flask is wrapped with aluminum foil, and the reaction mixture is stirred at room temperature for 22 h. The resulting orange mixture is transferred to a 500-mL separatory funnel and the reactor was rinsed twice with 25 mL of pentanes. The organic was washed with a saturated sodium thiosulfate solution (50 mL x 2). The combined aqueous layers are extracted with two 50 mL portions of pentanes and the combined organic phases are washed with 50 mL of saturated NaCl solution, dried over MgSO4, filtered, and concentrated by rotary evaporation (25 °C, 21 mmHg) to provide 8.58 g (100%) of 1-bromooct-1-yne as a reddish oil."
