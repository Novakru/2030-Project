General Procedure
Procedure for the Palladium-Catalyzed One-Pot Double Heck Coupling of 1 with Two Different Aryl Iodides (Table 1, Entry 2). To a solution of Pd2(dba)3•CHCl3 (7.8 mg, 0.0075 mmol, 2.5 mol%) and tri-2-furylphosphine (3.5 mg, 0.015 mmol, 5 mol%) in THF (1.0 mL) were added iodobenzene (62.7mg, 0.30 mmol), 1 (49.0 mg, 0.30 mmol), triethylamine (91.0 mg, 0.90 mmol), and MS4A (10 mg) at room temperature under argon and the reaction mixture was stirred at 60 oC for 5 h. To this mixture was added 2-iodothiophene (88.2mg, 0.42 mmol) and the reaction mixture was stirred at 60 oC for 42 h. After the mixture was cooled to room temperature, the catalyst was removed by filtration through a short silica gel pad (EtOAc). The filtrate was evaporated, and the residue was chromatographed on silica gel (hexane/EtOAc = 10/1) to afford 4ab (66 mg, 68%) as pale yellow oil.
