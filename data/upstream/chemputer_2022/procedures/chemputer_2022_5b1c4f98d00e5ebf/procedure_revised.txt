To the mixture of benzyl alcohol (2 mL), sodium azide (2 g) and PPh3 (2 g) was added CCl4-DMF (10 mL) and the mixture was warmed to 90 °C.
Mixture was stirred for 1 hour, allowed to coold down to room temperature and water (5 mL) was added
After stirring for 10 minutes ether (25 mL) was added.
The mixture was transferred to separatory funnel and washed with water.
The organic phase was cooled to 0 °C, filtered, dried over anhydrous Na2SO4 and concentrated under reduced pressure.
