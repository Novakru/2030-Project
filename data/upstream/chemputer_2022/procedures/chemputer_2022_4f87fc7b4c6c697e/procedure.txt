
In air, two 2000-mL one-necked, round-bottomed flasks (24/40), equipped with a Teflon-coated egg-shaped magnetic stirbar (3/4? × 2?), are charged with a 
mixture of N,N'-1,4-bis(2,6-diisopropylphenyl)-1,4-diazabutadiene (2 × 43 g, 0.23 mol, 1.0 equiv) and 
paraformaldehyde (2 × 3.4 g, 0.23 mol, 1.0 equiv) in EtOAc (2 × 1.0 L). 

Both reaction flasks are equipped with 50-mL pressure-equalizing addition funnels , which are charged with a 
solution of TMSCl (2 × 15 mL, 2 × 13 g, 0.23 mol, 1.0 equiv) in EtOAc (2 × 15 mL) and sealed with a rubber septum. 
The reaction flasks are placed in an oil bath, which is preheated at 70 °C. 
After the reaction mixtures are stirred at 70 °C for 10 min, the TMSCl solutions are added dropwise over 45 min with the external temperature maintained between 65 °C and 75 °C. 
The resulting mixtures are stirred at 70 °C for an additional 2 h. 
The flasks are then removed from the oil bath and cooled in an ice-water bath for ca. 20 min to allow the internal temperature of the reaction mixture to reach 15 °C. 

The resulting solids in the two flasks are subsequently combined and collected by filtration, 
rinsed with EtOAc (3 × 150 mL) in a glass fritted funnel and transferred into a 500-mL one-necked round-bottomed flask (24/40). 
After evaporation of the volatiles on a rotary evaporator (35 mmHg, 40 °C, 20 min), 
the solid is further dried by heating in an oil bath (preheated at 150 °C) at 150 °C under vacuum for 10 h to afford 73 g of compound 2 as a pinkish solid (75%).
