To a solution of allylamine (1.0 mmol) in MeOH (1.0 mL) was added
aldehyde (1.0 mmol), and the reaction mixture was stirred at room
temperature for 10 min. Acid (1.0 mmol) was added to the reaction mixture.
After the mixture was stirred for another 5 min, isocyanide (1.0 mmol)
was added. The reaction mixture was stirred overnight. Solvent was removed
under reduced pressure, and the residue was purified by flash chromatography
(petroleum ether/EtOAc/CH2Cl2) to give the corresponding Ugi
product.
