Allyl bromide(0.24mL,2.75mmol,1.1equiv.) was added to a suspension of 
heteroaryl thiol(2.5mmol,1.0equiv.) and potassium   carbonate(0.7g,5.0mmol,2.0equiv.)
in acetone(12.5mL,0.2 M)  and  the  reaction  was  stirred at  room  temperature  
until  completion.  
The mixture  was  filtered  and  concentrated  under  reduced  pressure.
The  crude  sulfide  was  taken into the next step without further purification.

The     crudematerial was dissolved     in     DCM     (12.5mL,0.2M)     
and mCPBA (1.55g,~77wt%,7.5mmol,3.0equiv.)  was  added portion  wise  
and  the  mixture  was  stirred at  room  temperature  until  completion.  
The  reaction  was  quenched  with  saturated  NaHCO3(15mL) and  extracted  with  DCM(15mL).  
The  combined  organic  layers  were  dried  over MgSO4
and  concentrated  under  reduced  pressure. 
The  product  was  purified  by  column chromatography.

General procedure A was followed using 
5-(trifluoromethyl)pyridine-2-thiol (447.5mg,2.5mmol,1.0equiv.). 
The  product  was  obtained  as  a  white  solid (583.6mg, 93%)
