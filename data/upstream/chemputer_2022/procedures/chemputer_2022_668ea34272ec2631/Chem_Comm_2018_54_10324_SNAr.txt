To a 10 mL sealed tube containing 3-methoxypyridine (1a) (55.0 mg, 0.504 mmol), NaH (101 mg, 2.53 mmol), and LiI (134 mg, 1.00 mmol) in THF (500 μL) was added piperidine (2a) (98.8 μL, 1.00 mmol) at 0 °C under a N2 atmosphere. The tube was sealed and the solution was then stirred at 60 °C for 8 h. The reaction mixture was quenched with cold water at 0 °C and the organic materials were extracted thrice with CH2Cl2. The combined organic extracts were washed with brine, dried over MgSO4, filtered and concentrated in vacuo. The resulting crude material was purified by flash column chromatography (hexane : EtOAc =3:1) to yield 3aa (72.1 mg, 0.444 mmol) in 88% yield as a yellow oil.
