A solution of requisite acetophenone (1.0 equiv.) and trifluoromethylsilane (1.2 equiv.) in THF (0.4 M)
was treated with TBAF (1 M in THF, 1 mol%) dropwise and the reaction mixture stirred for 5 min at rt.
The reaction was then concentrated in vacuo, the residue was filtered through a silica plug (eluent
pentane) and concentrated in vacuo to give tertiary trifluoromethyl trimethylsilyl ethers.

A solution of tertiary trifluoromethyl trimethylsilyl ether in methanol (0.5 M) was treated with aq. 3 M
HCl (equal volume) and the solution stirred for 16 h at rt. Once complete Et2O (equal volume) was
added and the layer separated, the aqueous layer was extracted with Et2O (2 × equal volume), the
combined organic layers were washed with brine, dried over MgSO4 and concentrated in vacuo to give
the tertiary alcohol.

Alternative hydrolysis:
... after 5 minutes, another portion of TBAF (1 M in THF, 1 equiv.) was added to remove the TMS group. The reaction was then concentrated in vacuo and the residue was purified by flash column chromatography (2-5% Et2O/pentane) to give the title product as a colourless oil.
