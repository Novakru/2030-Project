A dry reactor was charged with 2,3-O-isopropylidene-L-threitol (25.0 g, 0.154 mol). The reactor was evacuated and backfilled with argon three times. DCM (308 mL) (Note 3) and pyridine (37.4 mL, 0.462 mol) (Note 4) were added, and the stirred solution was cooled to 0°C with an ice-water bath. Methanesulfonyl chloride (29.8 mL, 0.385 mol) (Note 5) was added over a period of 10 min. After an additional 30 min, the ice-water bath was removed, and the stirred solution was stirred for 6 hr at 25 °C. A  precipitate formed (pyridinium chloride). Then  an aqueous saturated solution of sodium bicarbonate (300 mL) was added slowly, which dissolved the precipitate.  The solution was stirred for a further 30 min. The aqueous layer was extracted with DCM (3 × 100 mL). The organic layers were combined, dried over MgSO4 and concentrated in vacuo to give a tan solid. The product was dried for 30 min at 50 °C. The product was dissolved in ethanol (250 mL). 
Then methanesulfonic acid (0.204 mL, 3.14 mmol) (Note 9) was added and the reaction was refluxed for 10 hr and then cooled to 0°C for 1 h with an ice-water bath resulting in the formation of crystals. The crystals were collected by filtration. The filter cake was rinsed with cold ethanol (2 × 50 mL) and diethyl ether (2 × 50 mL). The filter cake was dried in a vacuum for 4 hr to give the product as white crystals, mp 101-102°C, 29.6 g, (84% yield) (Note 10).
