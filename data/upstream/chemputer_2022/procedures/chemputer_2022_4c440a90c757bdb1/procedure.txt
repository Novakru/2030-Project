B. (R)-4-Phenyl-3-(2-triisopropylsilyl-ethynyl)oxazolidin-2-one (3). The reaction vessel was charged with K2CO3 (2.79 g, 20.0 mmol, 2.0 equiv) (Note 9) and CuSO4•5H2O (252 mg, 1.00 mmol, 0.10 equiv) (Note 10).  Then  a solution of 1,10-phenanthroline (364 mg, 2.00 mmol, 0.20 equiv) (Note 11) in toluene (1.85 mL), a solution of 1-bromo-2-triisopropylsilylacetylene (2) (2.64 g, 10.0 mmol, 1.0 equiv.) (Note 7) in toluene (3.71 mL) and a solution of R-phenyloxazolidinone (1.65 g, 10.0 mmol, 1.0 equiv) in toluene (3.71 mL) were added . The flask was fitted with a reflux condenser topped with a septum and N2 inlet, and heated in an oil bath at 75 °C (bath temperature) for 48 h. The reaction was monitored using TLC analysis (Note 12). Upon completion, the reaction mixture was cooled to room temperature and transferred to a rotavap through  a cartridge containing Celite and silica. The mixture was washed through with 50% EtOAc/hexanes (75 mL), and the filtrate was concentrated on a rotary evaporator (20 – 30 mmHg, 70 °C). The crude residue was purified using silica gel column flash chromatography (Note 13) to give ynamide 3 (29.5–32.7 g, 81–88%) (Notes 14 and 15) as a yellow oil.
