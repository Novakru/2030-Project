1) Add paraformaldehyde (48 g) to reactor.
2) Add water (110 mL) directly to reactor at default speed without stirring.
3) Add 1 N phosphoric acid (4 mL) directly to reactor at default speed with stirring at 250 RPM.
4) Heat/Chill reactor to 90 °C for 1.5 h with stirring at 250 RPM.
5) Heat/Chill reactor to 25 °C with stirring at 250 RPM. Temperature control is continued after the temperature has been reached.
6) Add triethyl phosphonoacetate (80 mL) directly to reactor at default speed with stirring at 250 RPM.
7) Stir reactor for 5 min at 250 RPM stopping stirring afterwards.
8) Heat/Chill reactor to 35 °C with stirring at 250 RPM. Temperature control is continued after the temperature has been reached.
9) Add potassium carbonate water solution (60 mL) directly to reactor over 60 min with stirring at 500 RPM.
10) Stir reactor for 5 min at 250 RPM stopping stirring afterwards.
11) Heat/Chill reactor to 20 °C with stirring at 250 RPM. Temperature control is continued after the temperature has been reached.
12) Add diethyl ether (200 mL) directly to reactor at default speed with stirring at 250 RPM.
13) Add brine (150 mL) directly to reactor at default speed with stirring at 250 RPM.
14) Extract contents of reactor with diethyl ether (3 x 100 mL). Transfer waste phase (bottom) to waste, and product phase (top) directly to separator.
15) Wash contents of separator with brine (2 x 100 mL). Transfer waste phase (bottom) to waste, and product phase (top) through magnesium sulfate to rotavap.
16) Evaporate contents of rotavap with pressure 440 mbar at temperature 50 °C for 30 min.
