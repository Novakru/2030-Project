Ethyl α-(hydroxymethyl)acrylate. (See (Note 1)). A 1000-mL, four-necked, round-bottomed flask is fitted with mechanical stirrer, 250-mL equalizing funnel, condenser, and thermometer. Paraformaldehyde (48 g, 1.6 mol), 1 N phosphoric acid (4 mL), and water (110 mL) were added and heated at 90°C for 1.5 hr to form a clear aqueous formaldehyde solution. This solution is cooled to room temperature. Triethyl phosphonoacetate (80 mL, 0.4 mol) is added to the flask and the solution is stirred at room temperature at 1000 rpm. A solution of potassium carbonate (60.7 g, 0.44 mol) in water (60 mL) is added at 35°C in 60 minutes. The temperature reaches 35–40°C and must be maintained at this level (with a water bath if necessary). Stirring is continued for 5 min at 35°C after the end of the addition; then the mixture (liquid–liquid heterogenous mixture) is cooled rapidly to 20°C. Diethyl ether (200 mL) and brine (150 mL) are added. After decantation, the mixture is extracted with ether (3 x 100 mL). The combined organic layers are washed with brine (2 x 100 mL) (Note 3) and dried over magnesium sulfate; the solvents are evaporated under reduced pressure and the remaining oil is distilled to give a fraction at 65–70°C (1 mm) that weighs 38.5–41.6 g (74–80%), nD20 1.4494. The hydroxy ester is of high purity as shown by GLC analysis (25-m silica capillary OV-1 column) and spectral data (Note 4) and (Note 5).
