Under  air, O-benzyl hydroxylamine hydrochloride (27.93 g, 0.175 mol, 1 equiv) and Na2CO3(37.1 g, 0.35 mol, 2 equiv) were weighed 
into a 2 L round bottom flask fitted with mechanical stirring. 
CH2Cl2 and distilled water (0.7 L, 4:1 v/v, 0.25 M) were added with stirring and the flask was submerged in an ice bath. 
After 20 minutes of stirring at 0 ?C, p-trifluoromethyl benzoyl chloride (26 mL, 0.175 mmol, 1 equiv) was added dropwise by syringe. 
The poorly soluble product rapidly appears. 
The reaction was warmed to room temperature and stirred for 5 hours. 
CH2Cl2 was removed by rotary evaporation, diethyl ether (300 mL) was added to the resulting H2O suspension, and the mixture was filtered. 
The white solid product was washed with  H2O  (100  mL)  and  ether  (100  mL)  and  dried  under  vacuum  to  afford  the title  compound(46.06 g, 0.156 mol, 89%)

To  a  stirred  suspension  of  hydroxamate O-Benzyl  ester  (27.16  g, 92 mmol) in CH2Cl2(200 mL) 
was added freshly prepared t-BuOCl (12.5 mL, 110.4 mmol, 1.2 equiv) in a single portion. 
The reaction was shielded from ambient light and stirred under nitrogen at room temperature for 2 hours (consumption of starting material by TLC). 
The solution was concentrated in vacuo to afford a residue that was suspended in hexanes (100mL) 
and concentrated in vacuo a second time to afford the N-chloroaminde compound as a yellow solid, which was of sufficient purity and immediately used in the next step.

To  a  suspension  of dry,  freshly  prepared sodium  pivalate  (14.84  g,  119.6  mmol,  1.3  equiv)  in degassed MeCN  (150  mL)  under  nitrogen  was  added  
a solution  of  N-chloroamide  in degassed MeCN (75 mL). 
The reaction was shielded from light and vigorously stirred for 2 hours at ambient temperature. 
Upon consumption of the N-chloroamide (monitored by TLC), the reaction mixture was poured into diethyl ether (250 mL) and 
filtered over a small silica plug. 
The plug was rinsed with additional diethyl ether (50 mL) and 
solvent was removed under reduced pressure to afford the anomeric amide as a pale yellow liquid (31.2 g, 78.5 mmol, 76% overall yield).
