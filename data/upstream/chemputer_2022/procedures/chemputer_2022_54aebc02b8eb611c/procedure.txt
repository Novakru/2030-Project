A reactor was charged with L-threitol 1,4-bismethanesulfonate (25.0 g, 0.0898 moles) and diethyl ether (180 mL). The mixture was stirred vigorously to form a suspension, and a solution of potassium hydroxide (11.6 g, 0.207 mol) (Note 11) in water (35 mL) was added dropwise via the addition funnel over a period of 15 min. The clear mixture was stirred for an additional 45 min at room temperature. The reaction mixture was transferred to a 500-mL separatory funnel. The reactor was rinsed with DCM (20 mL) . The aqueous phase was extracted with DCM (3 × 50 mL). The combined ether and methylene chloride extracts were dried with MgSO4, the sodium sulfate was removed by filtration, and the solution was concentrated to approximately 50 mL total volume by rotary evaporation. The concentrate was fractionally distilled through a 13-cm Vigreux distillation column at atmospheric pressure to give the product as a clear oil, bp 138-140°C, 6.3 g (81%) (Note 12) and (Note 13).
