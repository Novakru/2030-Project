A. Preparation of cyclopropane 1,l-dicarboxylic acid (1).  The reaction vessel was charged with  triethylbenzylammonium chloride (5.75 g, 25.0 mmol. 1.0 equiv.). The reaction vessel was charged with 1,2-dibromoethane (3.30 mL, 37.5 mmol, 1.5 equiv.) and diethyl malonate (3.83 mL). Finally the mixture was stirred vigorously while a 50 % aq NaOH solution (50 mL) was added rapidly. The reaction mixture was vigorously stirred for 2 h at 25 °C. The reaction mixture was cooled with an ice bath to 15 °C. Then 65 mL of concentrated hydrochloric acid was added dropwise. The temperature of the flask was maintained between 15 and 25 °C during acidification. The aqueous layer was poured into a 4-L separatory funnel. Water (15 mL) was used to rinse the flask.  Brine (100 mL) was added to the separating funnel. The mixture was extracted six times with 30 mL of ether. The combined organic phases were washed with 50 mL of brine. The combined organic extracts were dried with anhydrous magnesium sulfate (MgSO4). The solvent was evaporation to give 55.2 g of a semisolid residue. Toluene (10 mL) was added to the rotavap. The suspension was stirred for 5 min.  Solvent was removed form the rotavap. Filtration of this mixture gave 43.1–47.9 g (66–73%) of 1 as white crystals, mp 137–140 °C. The solvent was evaporated.  The content of the rotavap was dried for 30 min at 50 °C.
