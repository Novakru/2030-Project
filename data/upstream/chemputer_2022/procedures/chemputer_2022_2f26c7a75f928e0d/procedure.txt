Cholesta-3,5-diene. A 50-mL, two-necked, round-bottomed flask was equipped with a magnetic stirring bar and a reflux condenser with a nitrogen inlet at the top. A reactor  was charged with 1.044 g (2.00 mmol) of cholesta-3,5-dien-3-yl trifluoromethanesulfonate (1),  8.3 mg (0.04 mmol) of palladium acetate, and  21 mg (0.08 mmol) of triphenylphosphine. The reactor was evacuated and back-filled with argon three times. Then 1.44 mL (6.00 mmol) of tributylamine (Note 7),  and 3.1 mL of N,N-dimethylformamide were added. Then  400 mL of inert gas were added. Then 1.03 mL (4.0 mmol) of formic acid in DMF were added over 2–3 min. The resulting mixture was warmed in an oil bath at 60°C for 1 hr with continuous stirring under nitrogen. During this period the mixture turned black. The contents of the reactor were poured into 50 mL of 2 N hydrochloric acid and transferred to a separator.   The reactor was rinsed  with  25 mL of diethyl ether.  The reactor was rinsed  with  25 mL of diethyl ether. The phase were  extracted with two 75-mL portions of ethyl ether.   The combined organic phases were then washed with 62 mL of 2 N hydrochloric acid, 62 mL of  saturated sodium bicarbonate solution and 2 x 100 mL of brine. The organic phase was then dried over anhydrous magnesium sulfate. The ether was evaporated at reduced pressure.
