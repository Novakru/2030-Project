Primary   amine   (1.2   equiv)   was   added   to  
a   suspension   of   2,4,6-triphenylpyrylium tetrafluoroborate (1.0 equiv) 
and EtOH (1.0 M) in a round-bottomed flask. 
The flask was fitted with a reflux condenser. 
The mixture was stirred and heated at reflux in an oil bath at 80–85 ºC for 4    h. 
The mixture was then allowed  to  cool  to  room temperature. 
If  product  precipitation occurred during reflux, 
the solid was filtered, washed with EtOH (3 x 25 mL) and then Et2O    (3 x 25  mL),  
and  dried  under  high  vacuum.  
If product  precipitation  did  not  occur  during  reflux, 
 the solution  was  diluted  with  Et2O  (2–3x  volume  of  EtOH  used)  
and  vigorously  stirred  for  1  h  to induce trituration. 
The resulting solid pyridinium salt was filtered and washed with Et2O (3 x 25 mL).
